CA Natasha Rajvaidya • Nov 4, 2025 • 4 min read

How modern Chartered Accountants have evolved from traditional number-crunchers and tax-filers into trusted strategic growth partners, virtual CFOs, and startup mentors guiding modern businesses.

Strategic Analysis & Practical Implications

In today's fast-evolving regulatory landscape, having absolute clarity on statutory mandates and financial positioning is the cornerstone of sustainable growth.
